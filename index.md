## Get Your Cert

Free practice questions for cloud, AI and MLOps certification exams, every answer explained.

**Website:** [getyourcert.org](https://getyourcert.org/), with practice tests, full-length mock
exams and study guides. No sign-up needed.

- [Study guides](https://getyourcert.org/guides): long-form articles on topics the exams test
  heavily, such as [VPC endpoints vs NAT gateways](https://getyourcert.org/guides/aws-vpc-endpoints-vs-nat-gateway)
  and [RAG vs fine-tuning](https://getyourcert.org/guides/rag-vs-fine-tuning)
- Practice questions by provider: [AWS](https://getyourcert.org/provider/aws),
  [Azure](https://getyourcert.org/provider/azure), [Google Cloud](https://getyourcert.org/provider/gcp),
  [Kubernetes (CNCF)](https://getyourcert.org/provider/cncf),
  [Generative AI](https://getyourcert.org/provider/genai), [MLOps](https://getyourcert.org/provider/mlops)
- Popular exams: [AWS Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)](https://getyourcert.org/cert/aws-saa-c03),
  [AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)](https://getyourcert.org/cert/aws-clf-c02),
  [Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)](https://getyourcert.org/cert/az-900),
  [Azure Administrator (AZ-104)](https://getyourcert.org/cert/az-104),
  [Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)](https://getyourcert.org/cert/cka),
  [Claude Certified Architect, Foundations](https://getyourcert.org/cert/claude-cca-f)

**Android app:** the same questions, fully offline.
