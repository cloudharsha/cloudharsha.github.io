# Privacy Policy — Get Your Cert

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

This policy covers the **Get Your Cert** Android app (package `com.harshareddy.getyourcert`),
distributed on Google Play, and this website.

## Summary

Get Your Cert does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app
works entirely offline.

## What the app stores

The app saves your practice-test results (which tests you have taken and your scores) **only on
your device**, using the device's local app storage. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not sent to us or to any third party,
- is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-up, or login.
- No analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.
- No advertising or advertising identifiers.
- No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
- No network requests — all questions are bundled inside the app.

## Children

The app is a study tool for professional certifications and is not directed at children. Since it
collects no data, it collects none from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated"
date.
